FAQ

Questions You Might Have

Do I need to pay to become a partner? +
No upfront platform fee is required. We keep the partner onboarding process simple, transparent, and focused on qualified referral collaboration.
How is commission calculated? +
You earn 20% of the order price every time your referred friend makes a purchase. Once bound, it is lifetime — you keep earning on every repeat purchase.
Is this multi-level marketing? +
No. It is single-level only. You earn from your direct referrals only. Your referred friends can also become partners, but their referrals do not affect you.
When do I receive my commission? +
7 days after the client completes service or confirms delivery (T+7), commission is credited to your account. You can withdraw once balance reaches $50.
What withdrawal methods are available? +
Bank transfer, PayNow, Alipay, and WeChat Pay. Processing takes 1–3 business days.
Is there a commission cap? +
No cap whatsoever. The more you refer, the more you earn. Recurring purchases mean recurring income.
