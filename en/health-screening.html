What Is Japan Precision Check-up

Ningen Dock —
A Life-saving Upgrade from Ordinary Check-ups

Japan's Precision Check-up (Ningen Dock) is not an ordinary health check — it's a comprehensive health evaluation system centered on early cancer screening, covering cardiovascular, metabolic, endocrine, and all major body systems.

Whether or not you have symptoms, Japan's precision check-up uses CT, MRI, ultrasound, PET-CT, and other high-precision equipment to thoroughly scan your body — detecting hidden health risks before symptoms appear. This is the core secret behind Japan's consistently top-ranked cancer cure rates.

Japan's check-up goes beyond a report — experienced specialists interpret each result item-by-item and provide personalized health management advice, giving you true knowledge of your own health.

FAQ

Japan Precision Check-up FAQ

Know the full process before you depart

How many days do I need to stay in Japan for a check-up? +
A basic precision check-up package can usually be completed in 1 day, covering imaging and biochemical tests. Packages including gastroscopy/colonoscopy require 2 days. PET-CT whole-body cancer screening can be completed in a single day; adding cardiovascular screening or genetic testing recommends 3–4 days. JMedPass will plan the most efficient itinerary for your needs.
Is the radiation from PET-CT safe? +
PET-CT radiation is equivalent to approximately 10–15 chest X-rays — within medically acceptable limits. Japan's PET-CT equipment uses high-precision, low-dose scanning technology that poses no significant health risk. Pregnant or breastfeeding women are advised against this scan and may choose alternative imaging.
The report is in Japanese — how will I understand it? +
JMedPass provides complete Chinese-language report translation and arranges bilingual consultants to accompany physician interpretations. All check-up results, recommendations, and follow-up instructions are explained in detail in Chinese — no language barrier.
If an issue is found, what happens next? +
If suspicious findings are identified, JMedPass assists you in booking the appropriate Japanese specialist for further assessment. If treatment is needed, we connect you with our partner centers in oncology, cardiology, neurology, and more. We also help forward reports to your domestic physician for seamless continuity.
What is included in the check-up package fee? +
Package fees typically include: check-up items (blood tests, imaging, endoscopy, etc.), physician interpretation, Chinese report translation, and consultant accompaniment. Flights and hotel are not included, but JMedPass assists with recommendations and bookings. Clear written quotes are provided during consultation — no surprise charges.
