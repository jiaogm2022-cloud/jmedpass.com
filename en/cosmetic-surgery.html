Why Japan Aesthetics

Rigorous Standards Create
World-Leading Aesthetic Medicine

Japan's aesthetic medicine is world-renowned for "natural, precise, and safe" results. Every practicing plastic surgeon must complete 10+ years of surgical training and pass the Japan Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery board certification before operating independently.

Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare enforces the world's most rigorous standards — all surgical materials, equipment, and procedures must be PMDA-certified. Every surgery you undergo in Japan is backed by national-level quality assurance.

JMedPass partners deeply with leading hospitals in Ginza and Omotesando, matching you with the ideal specialist while providing professional medical interpretation and concierge support — so you can confidently pursue your ideal self abroad.

FAQ

Japan Aesthetic Surgery FAQ

Answers to the most common questions from our clients

How is safety guaranteed for Japan aesthetic surgery? +
Japan's plastic surgeons complete 10+ years of surgical training and pass the Japan Society of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery board exam before practicing independently. All consumables and implants must be PMDA-certified. Sakura's partner hospitals are 100% properly credentialed — you can verify physician licenses and hospital certifications before surgery.
How much does Japan aesthetic treatment cost? +
Costs vary by procedure and hospital tier. Injectable treatments start from a few thousand yuan; facial surgeries typically range RMB 20,000–80,000; skeletal procedures are priced higher. Sakura guarantees transparent pricing with zero hidden charges and provides detailed cost breakdowns during consultation.
How long do I need to stay in Japan? +
Minimally invasive injectables usually allow return within 1–3 days. Facial surgeries generally require 7–10 days (including pre-op, surgery, and initial recovery). Skeletal procedures recommend 14+ days. Your consultant will tailor the exact itinerary to your procedure.
What about the language barrier? +
Sakura provides full Chinese-language translation and accompaniment — airport pickup, hospital registration, pre-op consultation to post-op care. All physician instructions and reports are translated into Chinese, ensuring you are fully informed and comfortable throughout.
Can unsatisfactory results be corrected? +
Japan's surgeons are renowned for precision, achieving 98% satisfaction. If there are any concerns, we help communicate with the Japanese attending physician for revision evaluation. We also have extensive experience serving clients seeking to correct unsatisfactory procedures done elsewhere.
