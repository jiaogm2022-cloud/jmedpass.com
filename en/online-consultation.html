FAQ

Remote Specialist Consultation FAQ

Key questions about remote second opinion and specialist consultation coordination

Are the doctors fully licensed and certified? +
Yes. All participating doctors hold a physician licence issued in Japan and have completed full specialty training. The platform is operated through a compliant Japanese medical institution under Japanese medical regulations, and you can review the doctor's licence information before your consultation.
Which regions do you deliver prescriptions to? +
We currently deliver to mainland China, Hong Kong, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia, and other major Asian regions. Specific medications available vary by destination import regulations — we will advise during your consultation. Note that certain controlled substances may not be internationally mailable, and the doctor will provide alternatives in those cases.
How is the consultation fee charged? +
Consultation fees vary by specialty and duration, generally ranging from CNY 300–800. Follow-up service fees, where applicable, are disclosed before confirmation. Your advisor will confirm the applicable consultation scope and fee structure before booking.
What conditions are suitable for online consultation vs. in-person? +
Online consultation is suitable for chronic disease management, common illness consultations, repeat prescriptions, AGA hair loss, weight management, skin issues, and other non-emergency needs. For emergencies (severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, serious trauma, etc.) or conditions requiring physical examination or surgery, please visit your local hospital emergency department immediately.
What languages can I consult in? +
Consultations are available in Chinese (Mandarin / Cantonese), Japanese, and English. Most patients communicate directly and fluently with doctors in Chinese without a translator. All consultation records, prescription instructions, and medication guidance can be provided in Chinese.
Remote consultation decision guide

Use Cases, Process, Cost and Documents

Remote consultation is suitable for non-emergency review and second opinions. It does not replace emergency care, physical examination or procedures that must be done offline.

Who is it for?

People who want to confirm direction before Japan travel, request a second opinion, organize complex records or receive medical interpretation support.

How does the process work?

Submit records, translate and summarize medical information, match specialty, book doctor, attend remote consultation, receive summary and next-step suggestions.

What is the cost range?

Fees depend on specialty, doctor profile, record complexity, translation volume and consultation duration. Scope and quotation are confirmed before booking.

Risk boundaries

Remote opinions are limited by record completeness and may not produce a final diagnosis. Emergency symptoms, procedures, imaging and some prescriptions require offline care.

Common questions

You can prepare question lists, translated reports and a timeline. Diagnosis, medication changes and treatment plans require doctor review of complete information.

Document checklist

Main complaint, diagnosis and timeline; reports, imaging, pathology or prescriptions; prior treatments; current medication; key questions for the doctor.

For chest pain, breathing difficulty, loss of consciousness, severe trauma or other emergency signs, contact local emergency services first.
