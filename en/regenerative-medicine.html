What Is Regenerative Medicine

Restarting the Body's Self-healing Program
Deep Repair at the Cellular Level

Regenerative medicine is one of the most cutting-edge fields of 21st-century medicine. Its core concept: the human body possesses remarkable repair and regeneration capacity, and stem cells are the "key" to unlocking this potential.

Stem cells can differentiate into various tissue cells, continuously replenishing and repairing damaged organs. Through three infusion sessions, approximately one billion highly active stem cells are introduced into the body, producing significant effects in anti-aging, joint repair, and metabolic improvement.

Japan is one of the world's most strictly regulated and technically mature countries for regenerative medicine. All stem cell treatment institutions must hold a "Plan Number" issued by the MHLW, with strict legal constraints on culture standards, passage counts, and sterility systems.

FAQ

Regenerative Medicine FAQ

Understand stem cell therapy — become an informed patient

Is stem cell therapy safe? Are there side effects? +
Autologous stem cells (from your own body) are used, eliminating theoretical immune rejection. Japan's certified institutions culture cells in Class 100 aerospace-grade sterile cleanrooms, keeping infection risk extremely low. Mild post-infusion fever or fatigue are normal immune responses that typically resolve within 1–2 days.
How long do the effects of regenerative medicine last? +
Results vary by individual constitution, disease state, and course completeness. Anti-aging courses typically sustain youthful effects for 3–5 years after three standard sessions. Joint repair and metabolic improvements tend to appear relatively quickly and deepen over time. Annual maintenance of 1–2 sessions is recommended.
How many sessions are needed? How often? +
The standard anti-aging course consists of 3 infusions approximately 1 month apart. Joint repair courses are typically 2–3 sessions depending on cartilage damage. For chronic disease support, frequency should be discussed with your attending physician. We adjust subsequent sessions based on your response after the first course.
What age group is best suited for stem cell regenerative medicine? +
Anti-aging stem cell therapy is generally recommended from age 35, when cell viability is still good for building cellular reserves. Disease-treatment stem cell therapy (joints, diabetes, etc.) has no specific age limit — suitability is determined by disease state and physical condition. We advise on eligibility after initial assessment.
